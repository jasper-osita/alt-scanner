Model Guide
Scan results appear here.
Rating blends the 20/50/100 stack on 1H (30%), 4H (40%) and Daily (30%), plus a nudge for a fresh AOTS break; two or more tangled timeframes always read Chop. Wick % is the share of each candle that is wick, averaged over the last 48 1H and 42 4H candles: under 50% is clean, over 65% (or 3+ spikes larger than 1.5× ATR) is wicky. Day score: 1H structure 40, relative volume now (last 6 hours vs the same hours on the previous 7 days) 20, volatility fit 15, strength vs BTC over 24h 15, entry location (distance from the 1H 20) 10. Swing score: 4H and Daily structure 50, daily relative volume 20, volatility fit 15, strength vs BTC over 7 days 15. Boards keep at most 2 coins per sector. Picks are a watchlist, not entries. RSI is the 14-period Wilder RSI on 1H, 4H and Daily: 70+ momentum, 60–70 pre-momentum, 40–60 neutral, 30–40 pre-weak, 30 or below weak; divergence compares the last two swing highs (or lows) with RSI; ignition is a cross of 60 (or 40) in the last 3 candles. Momentum score: RSI alignment 40, volume now 20, strength vs BTC 20, range expansion 20, minus 10 for a divergence against the move. Position sizing, the watchlist, notes, alerts and the scorecard are saved in this browser only. Alerts fire while the app is open. Charts by TradingView Lightweight Charts™ (Apache 2.0). Data: Binance and Bybit public APIs, read in your browser. Logos: TradingView.
