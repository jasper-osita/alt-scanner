Model Guide
Scan results appear here.
Rating blends the 20/50/100 stack on 1H (30%), 4H (40%) and Daily (30%), plus a nudge for a fresh AOTS break; two or more tangled timeframes always read Chop. Wick % is the share of each candle that is wick, averaged over the last 48 1H and 42 4H candles: under 50% is clean, over 65% (or 3+ spikes larger than 1.5× ATR) is wicky. Day score: 1H structure 40, relative volume now (last 6 hours vs the same hours on the previous 7 days) 20, volatility fit 15, strength vs BTC over 24h 15, entry location (distance from the 1H 20) 10. Swing score: 4H and Daily structure 50, daily relative volume 20, volatility fit 15, strength vs BTC over 7 days 15. Boards keep at most 2 coins per sector. Picks are a watchlist, not entries. Position sizing, the watchlist, notes, alerts and the scorecard are saved in this browser only. Alerts fire while the app is open. Charts by TradingView Lightweight Charts™ (Apache 2.0). Data: Binance and Bybit public APIs, read in your browser. Logos: TradingView.
